Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Paweł Karmański, contact: shroomer.app@gmail.com.

Shroomer is an app for recording mushroom-picking trips: it records your route, saves the spots where you found mushrooms and shows them to other users of the app.

Giving an e-mail address is required to create an account. Everything else is voluntary — without it some features simply do not work (e.g. without location permission you cannot record a route).

Background location — why we ask for this permission

A mushroom-picking trip lasts for hours with the phone in your pocket and the screen off. Without permission to use location in the background, the track would break off after a moment, and the back to the car feature — which guides you back along your own track — would stop working. It is a safety feature in the forest, not a convenience.

Location is recorded and read in the background only during an ongoing trip, which you start yourself and end yourself. Once the trip ends, the app stops tracking your position in the background. How your phone shows that recording is on:

  • Android — a persistent notification in the notification bar. If you turn off notifications for Shroomer, recording keeps working, but Android 13 and newer then show it only in the list of active apps (pull down the notification shade → active apps), not in the bar itself.
  • iPhone — the blue location indicator in the status bar.
  • In the app itself, the recording screen shows the ongoing trip until you end it.

Location outside a trip — the map and "Near me"

Without a trip, too, the app reads your position, only while it is open and only if you have already allowed location access:

  • Map — when you open the Map tab, the app centres the map on your position, and maps (including in trip details) show a dot with your location.
  • Conditions map, "Near me" — finds forests within a radius of your position. The first search happens on its own when you turn on the conditions layer and location permission is already granted; later ones when you press "Near me".

These readings are not saved and do not leave your phone: the app filters and sorts forests on the phone, and downloads the list of forests and their scores in files that cover all of Poland. We do not send your coordinates to us, to Copernicus/ECMWF or to anyone else. The only trace is downloading the map pieces for the area you are viewing from Cloudflare (below).

Who sees your data — the most important part of this document

Shroomer is a social app. Your trips, with the route and find pins accurate to the metre, and photos of your finds are visible to other users of the app. How far this visibility extends depends solely on one setting in the app (Profile → Privacy):

  • Private account — the default for new accounts. Your trips can be seen only by people you have allowed yourself, by accepting their follow request. People you have already accepted keep access until you remove them.
  • Public account — only if you turn it on yourself. Your trips, routes, pins and photos can then be seen by every signed-in person, including someone who does not follow you: they can find your account by name, open it and look at everything. Following you then does not require your consent.

Regardless of this setting, your display name and number of trips are visible to every signed-in person — otherwise nobody could ask you for access.

Registration in the app is open. If you switch your account to public, a person you do not know can see exactly where you pick mushrooms. Accounts created before 17 September 2026 kept the setting they had — check it in Profile → Privacy.

Who we entrust data to

  • Supabase — database and photo storage. Servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Cloudflare (R2, address maps.shroomer.app) — hosting of the base map and of the conditions-map data. The app only downloads ready-made files from it; we do not send your account, name or location there. With every download, however, Cloudflare receives, like any server, your IP address and technical request data (the file address and the map piece it covers, the time, the app type). It processes them to deliver the files and protect the service against abuse, under its privacy policy. We have not turned on logging of these requests and we do not link them to your account. Sources and licences of the conditions-map data: data-sources.html.
  • Google — three things:
    • Gmail — sending sign-in e-mails (e.g. password reset). It sees your e-mail address and the content of that message.
    • Sign-in with a Google account, only if you choose it. Google confirms to us your e-mail address and your first and last name. We do not receive your Google account password.
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to Android phones. It sees your phone's notification token and the content of the notification (the name of the person who commented and the beginning of the comment).
  • Expo — acts as an intermediary in sending push notifications to Google. It sees the same as Firebase Cloud Messaging: the token and the content of the notification.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some providers are US companies and may process data outside the EEA:

  • Google, Expo and Cloudflare — on the basis of their certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (a European Commission adequacy decision), and outside its scope on the basis of standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.
  • Supabase — the data sits in Frankfurt, but the company is based in the USA; any access from outside the EEA is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement.

You can get a copy of these safeguards by writing to shroomer.app@gmail.com.

We do not pass data to anyone else and we do not sell it.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as the account exists. After it is deleted, the data disappears from our database immediately and irreversibly. We do not make database backups, and on the plan we use the provider (Supabase) does not make automatic backups — so there is no copy from which deleted data could be restored.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Privacy → Delete account (you have to press and hold the button).

Without the app: write to shroomer.app@gmail.com from the address the account is registered with. We delete it within 30 days. Details: account-deletion.html.

Deleting your account erases the account, the profile with its photo, all trips (except as described below), routes, raw GPS points, finds, all photos, comments, reactions and saved car positions. The operation cannot be undone — we have no trash bin and no copy from which it could be restored.

A joint trip you started is not deleted if other participants are in it — so that they do not lose their own finds. It passes to the participant who joined earliest (among those who have not left it or who added finds to it). Your finds, route and photos from that trip are deleted; its title and description stay, taken over by the new owner. If no such participant is left, the whole trip is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object. Write to shroomer.app@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland) if you believe that we process your data unlawfully.

Children

The app is not intended for persons under 16 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Photos used to train the model

The recognition model was trained on photos shared under Creative Commons licences. List of authors and licences: attribution.html. Photos of the app's users are currently not used to train the model.

Mushroom safety — disclaimer

The app shows species descriptions and warnings about similar species, compiled from published sources. They have not been verified by a mycologist and are not advice on edibility. Never eat a mushroom based on what the app shows.
