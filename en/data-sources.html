Data sources of the conditions map

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The fruiting-conditions map in the Shroomer app is computed from the weather, forest and soil data below. Each source has its own licence or terms; this page gives the required notices, citations and a description of our changes. Shroomer is not affiliated with or endorsed by any data provider. The map shows conditions, not the probability of finding mushrooms.

OpenStreetMap and the ODbL

Some published files combine OpenStreetMap data with other sources. They are derived databases under the ODbL 1.0 and we make each of them available, as a whole, under that licence. The files are at https://maps.shroomer.app/conditions/v2/static/ (listed with checksums in manifest.json, licence notice in LICENSE.txt); latest.json names the files of the current nightly run.

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL). https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Files made available under the ODbL 1.0:

  • static/forest-cells-*.pmtiles
  • static/forest-outline-*.pmtiles
  • static/forest-names-*.pmtiles
  • static/forests-*.json, static/forest-members-*.npz
  • static/restricted-*.pmtiles
  • runs/<run>/cells.pmtiles, runs/<run>/forests.json

The other files (e.g. overview.pmtiles, places-*, gminy-*, habitat, climatology) hold no OpenStreetMap data and are subject to their sources' terms.
