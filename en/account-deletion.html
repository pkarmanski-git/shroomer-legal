Deleting your account in the Shroomer app

This is a translation of the Polish original; if they differ, the Polish version applies. This page is available without signing in — it is also used by people who have already uninstalled the app. Privacy in general: privacy policy.

App name: Shroomer
Publisher: Paweł Karmański

Option one: in the app

  1. Open Shroomer and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Choose Privacy.
  4. At the bottom of the screen, press and hold the Delete account button.

The account disappears immediately. There is no e-mail confirmation and no grace period.

Option two: without the app

Write to shroomer.app@gmail.com from the address the account is registered with, with the subject “Account deletion”. We will delete it within 30 days of receiving the message.

What is deleted

Everything, irreversibly:

  • the account and profile (e-mail address, display name),
  • all trips, together with their routes and raw GPS points,
  • all finds, together with their precise coordinates,
  • all photos of finds,
  • saved car positions,
  • follow relationships in both directions.

What remains

Nothing. We do not keep data after an account is deleted — there is no trash bin and no backup from which it could be restored.

Database backups on the provider's side (Supabase) are overwritten in a cycle of up to 7 days and are not searched or restored by us.
