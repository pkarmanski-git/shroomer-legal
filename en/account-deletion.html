Deleting your account in the Shroomer app

This is a translation of the Polish original; if they differ, the Polish version applies. This page is available without signing in — it is also used by people who have already uninstalled the app. Privacy in general: privacy policy.

App name: Shroomer
Publisher: Paweł Karmański

Option one: in the app

  1. Open Shroomer and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Choose Privacy.
  4. At the bottom of the screen, press and hold the Delete account button.

The account disappears immediately. There is no e-mail confirmation and no grace period.

Option two: without the app

Write to shroomer.app@gmail.com from the address the account is registered with, with the subject “Account deletion”. We will delete it within 30 days of receiving the message.

What is deleted

Irreversibly:

  • the account and profile (e-mail address, display name, profile photo),
  • all trips, together with their routes and raw GPS points (exception: a joint trip — see below),
  • all finds, together with their precise coordinates, species and notes,
  • all photos of finds and photos added to trips,
  • saved car positions,
  • comments, reactions and reports,
  • follow relationships in both directions.

What remains

None of your data. We do not keep data after an account is deleted — there is no trash bin and no backup from which it could be restored. We do not make database backups, and on the plan we use the provider (Supabase) does not make automatic backups.

The only exception is a joint trip you started, if other participants are in it. It is not deleted, so that they do not lose their own finds: it passes to the participant who joined earliest (among those who have not left it or who added finds to it). Your finds, route and photos from that trip are deleted; its title and description stay, taken over by the new owner. If no such participant is left, the whole trip is deleted.
