Shroomer community terms

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This is a translation of the Polish original; if they differ, the Polish version applies. By creating an account you accept these terms. How we handle your data is described in a separate document: privacy policy.

Who can use Shroomer

Shroomer is for people aged 16 and over. One account belongs to one person; do not pretend to be someone else.

What you must not post

This covers everything other people can see: your display name, profile photo, trips, photos, notes and comments. Do not post:

  • insults, harassment, threats or content that demeans someone;
  • hate speech — attacks on someone because of their origin, nationality, religion, sex, orientation, disability or age;
  • sexual content, nudity or violence;
  • spam, advertising or links unrelated to the trip;
  • illegal content, including encouraging people to pick mushrooms where it is forbidden (national parks, nature reserves, military areas);
  • other people's personal data, including the places where someone else picks mushrooms, without their consent;
  • photos you do not have the rights to.

Mushroom safety

Do not present a mushroom as edible in the app unless you are sure. Species descriptions and recognition suggestions in the app are not advice on edibility and have not been verified by a mycologist.

Reporting and blocking

If something breaks these terms, report it in the app: on a comment, on a trip or on the person's profile. You can also block a user — they then cannot comment on or react to your trips. You can unblock people in Profile → Privacy.

If you cannot use the app, write to shroomer.app@gmail.com.

How we moderate

A person reviews every report, usually within 48 hours. We remove content that breaks these terms. For serious or repeated violations we suspend the account, and for illegal content we may notify the relevant authorities. The reported person is not told who reported them.

Your content

What you post stays yours. You allow us to store it and show it to other users, within the privacy settings you choose, for as long as your account exists.

Changes to these terms

We will announce significant changes in the app; you will need to accept the changed terms again. The date of the last change is at the top of this document.
